Why there's still no per-seat plan

TestAIx exists to give QA teams better tools. Charging per tester would turn every new hire into a budget debate and push people to share logins or skip the release review.

So even at launch prices, the price follows usage. Testers, developers, product managers and support leads all look at the same evidence.

Launch pricing questions

How long does launch pricing last?

Until January 2027, and only for the next 100 users who sign up. When either runs out, the launch prices come off this page.

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. Two applications, 15 pages explored, 500 execution credits (50 test cases) and 5 execution runs, with no card and no end date. Need more credits? Upgrade to Individual.

What happened to the Scale plan?

It's now Enterprise. Larger teams get unlimited applications and allowances set to their needs, priced after a short conversation with sales.

Do you charge per seat?

No. There are no seat licenses on any plan, including launch pricing. Adding testers, developers or product managers doesn't change the price.

Does unlimited generation mean unlimited execution?

No. Writing test cases is unlimited on paid plans and limited on Free. Running them uses execution credits from your allowance.

Who is Captain Croak?

The bug-eating frog inside TestAIx. He walks your app's journeys, catches what breaks and shows your team the evidence. You decide what ships.
