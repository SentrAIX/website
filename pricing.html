Why there's no per-seat plan

TestAIx exists to give QA teams better tools. Charging per tester would turn every new hire into a budget debate and push people to share logins or skip the release review.

Pricing questions

What is an execution credit?

Credits are how we count the work the AI does when it runs your tests. One test run uses 10 credits, so 1,000 credits cover 100 test cases. Extra credits are added in blocks of 1,000.

Do you charge per seat?

No. There are no seat licenses on any plan. You pay for applications or projects, pages explored and execution credits, so adding testers, developers or product managers doesn't change the price.

Can I start free?

Yes. Basic includes 2 applications, 10 pages explored a month, 100 execution credits a month (10 test cases), 1 execution run, unlimited AI test case generation and community support.

Does unlimited generation mean unlimited execution?

No. Writing test cases is unlimited on every plan. Running them uses execution credits from your allowance.

What does "unlimited executions" mean?

Team and Scale don't cap the number of runs. Included credits and extra-credit rates still apply, and Scale adds a priority queue.

Can Individual buy extra credits?

An extra-credit rate hasn't been set for Individual. If you expect more than 500 credits a month, talk to us first.

Do prices include Azure infrastructure?

Confirm infrastructure, deployment responsibilities and any separate Azure costs with us for your chosen model.
