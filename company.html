Our story

Engineering teams can now build and release features faster than they can confidently validate them.

Traditional test automation means writing scripts, repairing fragile selectors, chasing flaky tests and maintaining all of it every time the application changes. We built SentrAIx around a different idea: testing should understand how your application really works, create meaningful tests from that, and adapt as the product evolves.

That idea became TestAIx, a governed autonomous validation platform. It helps engineering and quality teams create, run and maintain tests grounded in the business, with less scripting and less upkeep, while people keep the decisions.

Our vision: AI-native testing that anticipates, adapts and accelerates quality without manual friction.

Why the name

A sentry keeps watch so everyone else can get on with their work. A sentry doesn't run the place. That's the job we want our AI to do.

SentrAIx was founded in 2025 in Aurora, Illinois. TestAIx applies the idea to software releases: it keeps watch over the journeys your customers depend on, and your team decides what ships. More products will follow, built the same way.

How we work

  1. Curious by design

    We ask useful questions and challenge our own assumptions, the way good testers do.

  2. Honest about evidence

    A conclusion should match what was observed. We say what we don't know.

  3. People own the decision

    Our products help people decide. They don't decide for them.
