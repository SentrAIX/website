What is a test case execution?

It is how we count what you run: one test case executed once is one execution. Free includes 50, Individual 200 a month and Team 800 a month. Extra executions are $25 per 200 on Individual and Team; on Free, upgrade to Individual for more. Enterprise is sized to your requirements.

What is TestAIx?

The first product from SentrAIx. It explores your web application, drafts and runs test cases across real user journeys and shows your team the evidence before a release.

Which applications can we test?

Browser-based web applications that you are authorized to test. We review authentication, environment access and constraints with you before onboarding.

Do you work with Cloudflare and other bot protection?

We don't bypass bot protection such as Cloudflare, CAPTCHA or multi-factor sign-in. Those controls exist to keep automated access out, and we respect them. Point TestAIx at a test or staging environment that allows automated access: relax the bot rules there, allow-list our testing traffic, or give us a test account without a CAPTCHA or multi-factor step.

What happens when a test fails?

You get the steps, screenshots and diagnostics for that run. Your team investigates the behavior against the intended outcome and decides what it means.

Does a failed test always mean a bug?

No. The cause could be the application, the environment, the test data or the test itself. Look at the evidence before assigning blame.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. SentrAIx's published security policy states that customer data is not used for model training.

Can we request security documentation?

Yes. Bring your requirements and we'll walk through what's available and where the gaps are.

Can we choose where it runs?

Yes. Use hosted SaaS, or deploy TestAIx as an Azure Managed Application in your own subscription.

Can we start with a single workflow?

Yes, and we recommend it. A narrow evaluation tells you whether TestAIx is useful before you expand.

Can I start free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 2 applications, 5 pages per analysis at crawl depth 5, 50 test case executions, AI test case generation and automation, and community support. It is free forever, with no card needed.
