Governed autonomy starts with a boundary

Set scope, evidence and ownership before an AI touches your app.

All posts

Start with intent

Before choosing automation, name the decision it should support. "Can this user upgrade?" is more useful than "Can the agent click the checkout button?" The outcome tells your team what a good test has to observe.

Define a safe scope

Choose authorized environments, suitable test identities and the actions that are allowed. Treat credentials, data and irreversible operations as explicit parts of the plan. Start small rather than broad and unbounded.

Uncertainty is a reason to investigate, never a reason to guess.

Agree when a person steps in

Decide which outcomes need review, who owns that review and what evidence they need. Don't treat an agent's confident explanation as proof.

Keep evidence tied to the claim

Record what ran and what it observed. Keep a failed test separate from a confirmed defect until someone has looked.

Revisit the policy as the app changes

A new role, payment flow or integration can change the risk. Update permissions and review points alongside the scenarios.
