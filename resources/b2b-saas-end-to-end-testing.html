The risk lives between the screens

A business-first guide to testing onboarding, billing and permissions.

All posts

Define the business outcome

For onboarding, the outcome might be an activated workspace with the right owner. For billing, a subscription with the right entitlement. Write it down before turning it into checks.

Choose representative journeys

Start with paths that affect customer value or access: signup, invitations, role changes, plan changes and cancellation.

A payment confirmation is one observation, not the whole story.

Test boundaries on purpose

Use agreed test organizations and roles to probe tenant and permission boundaries. Check what should be allowed and what should be refused.

Connect payment to entitlement

Check that subscription status and product access agree after a payment. Then try a declined card, a retry, a downgrade and a cancellation.

Keep an evidence trail

Tie each result to a journey and an expected outcome. The goal is a release decision you can defend.

Pick the journey that worries you most. Start there.
