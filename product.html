Test the outcome a customer cares about.

A login page that loads is a start. A customer who signs up, pays and gets what they paid for is the thing worth checking.

  1. It finds the real workflows

    TestAIx explores navigation, roles and multi-step journeys, then shows you the map so you can agree on what matters.

  2. It writes the test cases

    Test cases are grounded in how your app actually behaves. Generation is unlimited on paid plans and limited on the free plan.

  3. It runs them in a real browser

    End to end, including declined cards, missing data and blocked roles.

  4. It shows its work

    Screenshots, step logs and diagnostics for every run, so a failure arrives with context.

How it works

Four steps. Your team owns the first one and the last one.

Step 1 of 4

Point it at your app

Share a staging URL, a couple of test logins and the ground rules. You decide what it may touch and what it must never do.

Step 2 of 4

It maps the territory

TestAIx explores pages, roles and flows like a new user would, then drafts test cases from what it found.

Step 3 of 4

It walks the journeys, again and again

Real browser runs through signup, billing, invites and permissions, including declined cards and expired sessions.

Step 4 of 4

Your team reads the evidence

Each run comes back with screenshots, steps and diagnostics. Your people decide what a failure means and whether to ship.

What it does well today

Built for web applications, with the controls a QA lead would ask for.

No per-seat pricing on any plan.

  1. Adaptive maintenance

    When a button moves or a label changes, TestAIx revisits the affected tests instead of failing on a stale selector. Your team reviews what changed.

  2. Scheduled and on-demand runs

    Run before a release or on a daily schedule on paid plans. Enterprise adds a priority queue.

  3. Governance boundaries

    Environments, test identities and blocked actions are set before execution and respected during it.

  4. SaaS or your Azure

    Hosted by us, or deployed as a managed application in your subscription.
