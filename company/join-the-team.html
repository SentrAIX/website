Sales Development Representative, GTM

Build qualified pipeline and help shape the go-to-market motion for TestAIx, governed autonomous testing for AI-accelerated engineering teams.

Apply for the SDR role
Open position · Go-to-market

The role

Turn a focused product story into qualified conversations.

You will work closely with SentrAIx leadership to identify teams where release confidence, test maintenance, and AI-accelerated delivery create an urgent need for governed autonomous testing. This is a hands-on role for someone who can research carefully, write clearly, learn quickly, and improve the motion from evidence.

What you will do

  • Research and prioritize accounts with a credible fit for TestAIx.
  • Run thoughtful outbound across email, LinkedIn, and calls without relying on generic volume.
  • Qualify engineering, QA, product, and release-confidence needs and book relevant discovery meetings.
  • Maintain clean pipeline data, follow-up discipline, and weekly activity and conversion reporting.
  • Bring market objections and buyer language back into messaging, content, and product feedback.

What we are looking for

  1. 01

    Clear, credible communication

    You can make a technical product relevant without exaggerating what it does.

  2. 02

    Disciplined prospecting

    You research before reaching out and personalize around business and engineering context.

  3. 03

    Learning mindset

    You test messaging, study conversion data, and improve the process continuously.

  4. 04

    Early-stage ownership

    You are comfortable building structure while executing the work yourself.

  5. 05

    Relevant exposure

    B2B SaaS, developer tools, software quality, Azure, or enterprise technology experience is valuable.

What success looks like

A repeatable, evidence-led GTM motion.

Success is not measured by activity alone. It means improving account selection, earning replies from relevant buyers, creating qualified meetings, learning why opportunities advance or stall, and turning those lessons into a stronger pipeline motion.

  1. 01

    Sharper targeting

    A clear view of which teams, triggers, and use cases produce genuine fit.

  2. 02

    Qualified conversations

    Meetings grounded in real validation, governance, and release-confidence needs.

  3. 03

    Measurable learning

    Messaging and outreach that improve through conversion evidence and buyer feedback.
